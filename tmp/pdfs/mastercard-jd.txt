Title and Summary
The Services team and solutions fuel growth for partners globally by providing cutting-edge capabilities in
Customer Acquisition and Engagement, Security Solutions, Business and Market Insights, and Open
Banking. Operating in a fast-paced, agile environment, our teams deliver end-to-end solutions for a
diverse global customer base including retailers, airlines, hotels, tourism agencies, public sector entities,
restaurants, consumer goods, and telecom companies.

Mastercard Identity, within the Security Solutions organization, leads the development of products and
services that enable global commerce, advance financial inclusion, prevent fraud, and power seamless
digital experiences. As part of Mastercard Identity, the Business Identity team is responsible for building
and managing products, programs, and services focused on risk assessment and business entity
verification. The team delivers critical value across the ecosystem, helping to combat emerging fraud
threats and ensure trusted, secure commerce across the network.

The Manager, Product Management – Technical, plays a key role in delivering scalable, interoperable
solutions that enable businesses to be securely identified and trusted across digital ecosystems. This role
is responsible for translating product strategy into well-defined technical requirements and driving
execution across cross-functional teams including engineering, data science, commercialization, and
legal. The Manager partners closely with internal stakeholders and external customers to refine
requirements, prioritize the product backlog, and deliver high-quality Business Identity capabilities that
enhance onboarding, reduce fraud, and support regulatory compliance. With a strong focus on execution,
technical rigor, and customer impact, the Manager ensures timely delivery of features and continuous
improvement of product capabilities aligned with Mastercard’s broader vision for digital trust infrastructure.


Role
The Manager, Product Manager - Technical will provide detailed technical analysis of product or
application hardware and software to ensure that business and functional requirements can be met.

• Translate product roadmap from high-level strategy into detailed technical requirements, planning, and
   prioritization.
• Analyze system requirements and elaborate them into features, user stories, and clear acceptance
   criteria within an Agile framework.
• Quickly develop a strong understanding of complex platform systems and contribute effectively.
• Elicit and define customer needs and business requirements, bringing clarity, structured thinking, and
    measurable product KPIs and metrics.
• Design features through prototypes and experiments, decompose requirements into user stories, and
    prioritize the product backlog.
• Collaborate closely with engineering and product teams to assess feasibility, align dependencies, and
    drive delivery of high-quality solutions.
• Continuously monitor feature development at the feature and epic levels to ensure progress and
    alignment.
•   Review product demos with development teams against defined acceptance criteria.
•   Identify gaps in requirements and manage dependencies across teams.
•   Understand and evaluate technical implications and impacts of product requirements.
•   Lead joint application design sessions and technical story reviews.
•   Collaborate with software engineers to ensure strong user story comprehension and execution.
•   Partner with quality engineering teams to validate expected outcomes and product quality.
•   Work closely with stakeholders and product partners to gather requirements and maintain a
    well-prioritized backlog.
• Regularly engage stakeholders to understand evolving needs and incorporate feedback into the
    backlog.
•   Facilitate product demos, handoffs, and post-launch reviews with stakeholders.
•   Drive continuous improvement by incorporating learnings from releases and feedback loops.
•   Clearly document requirements using rules, workflows, diagrams, and state transition models.
•   Ensure timely identification and remediation of vulnerabilities and technical risks.
•   Support regulatory and compliance requirements, including audit evidence preparation and product risk
    assessments.
